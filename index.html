Core Idea

Create a virtual biological copy—a Digital Twin—that mirrors real organisms and predicts outcomes across agriculture, health, and environment.

System Modules

The system is built from seven main modules that transform raw genomic and environmental data into actionable predictions.

1. Data Collection Layer

Collects DNA sequences, crop metrics, soil data, weather readings and disease records as the raw input layer.

2. AI Learning Engine

Uses machine learning, deep learning, and pattern recognition to identify gene functions, crop traits, and environmental effects.

3. Genomic Mapping System

Translates raw DNA into structured gene behavior maps, such as resistance, yield risk, and drought tolerance.

4. Digital Twin Generator

Builds virtual biological models that simulate plants, genes, and organisms inside a digital environment.

5. Simulation Engine

Runs experiments on crop and health models using heat, drought, disease, and gene-editing scenarios.

6. Prediction System

Delivers outputs such as crop variety recommendations, yield forecasts, disease alerts, and personalized health insights.

7. Security & Ethics Layer

Protects sensitive genetic data with encryption, access control, authentication, and ethical AI monitoring.
